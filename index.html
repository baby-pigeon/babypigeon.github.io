We put AI to work in your business

Baby Pigeon is a small, client-focused applied AI studio in Vancouver and Montreal. We help companies find problems that can be fixed with AI and deliver the solutions: agents, assistants and product features that run on your data and fit the way your team already works.

Have a task in mind? Tell us about it.

What we do

  1. AI agents

    Software that takes a task from start to finish: reads the request, looks things up in your systems, prepares the result and checks with a person before anything important goes out. Every agent we build has clear limits and a log you can actually read.

    For example: support triage, lead research, order and invoice handling, internal reporting.

  2. Assistants for your documents

    Ask questions of your manuals, policies, contracts or internal wiki and get answers with a link to the exact page they came from. Built on your own documents, with your access rules respected.

    For example: technical standards, onboarding handbooks, product specs, sales knowledge.

  3. AI features in your product

    Summaries, search that understands what people mean, auto-tagging, writing help, recommendations. We add them to the app you already have, in your stack, with running costs you can predict.

  4. Document and data processing

    Turning PDFs, scans, forms and emails into clean, structured data. Worth a look if someone on your team spends hours a week copying numbers from one place into another.

  5. Prototypes

    Not sure an idea will hold up? We build a small working version and try it on your real data, so the decision to go further is based on how it performs rather than how it sounds.

  6. Evaluation and fixes

    Already running something built on a language model? We measure how often it gets things right, find where it breaks and fix it: test sets, guardrails, model choice, speed and cost.

  7. Advice and training

    Working sessions with your team on what current models can and can't do, where they would help in your day-to-day work, and how to use them without putting your data at risk.

How we work

The problem comes first
We start with the task and what a good result looks like. Sometimes a spreadsheet or a simple script will do the job, and we'll tell you so.
Start small
The first version is small and tested on your data. It grows once it has shown it's useful.
You see progress every week
Short cycles with something working to look at, so there are no surprises at the end.
It's yours
You get the code, the prompts and the documentation. We pick the model for the job, from commercial APIs to open models you can host yourself.
